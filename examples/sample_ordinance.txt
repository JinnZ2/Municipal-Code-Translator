ORDINANCE NO. 2026-42 - ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT (ADU) REGULATION

PURPOSE AND INTENT: The intent of this ordinance is to expand affordable housing supply and mitigate residential density shortages in accordance with State Housing Element Law.

Section 1. Authorization.
An accessory dwelling unit shall be permitted on any lot in an R-1 district containing an existing single-family residence. No person shall construct an accessory dwelling unit exceeding a maximum height restriction of 16 feet. A conditional use permit shall be required where setback requirements of less than 4 feet are proposed.

Section 2. Compliance and Fees.
Pursuant to Chapter 17.04 of the Municipal Code and in accordance with IBC 2024 standards, a building permit shall be required prior to construction. The applicant shall pay a non-refundable application fee of $350, plus a plan review fee of $0.75 per sq. ft. and an impact fee of 0.5% of project valuation.

Section 3. Review.
The Planning Department shall render a decision within 60 days of a complete submittal. Questions may be directed to the Planning Department at (555) 123-4567 or permits@example.gov.
